# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

Interactions
- Pair with Product Managers for requirements and acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with QA on test plans and validations
- Work with Release Manager on deployment steps

---

## Product Managers (PdM)

Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

Interactions
- Drive acceptance criteria for backlog items
- Work with Data Analysts to define metrics and dashboards
- Align with PMs and Release Managers for deliverable timing

---

## Project Managers (PM)

Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting

Interactions
- Serve as primary coordinator between PdMs, Developers, QA, and stakeholders
- Escalate blockers to Product Lead or Sponsor as needed

---

## QA / Testing

Role Summary
QA ensures delivered functionality meets acceptance criteria and quality standards.

Responsibilities
- Define test plans and acceptance criteria validation
- Execute manual and automated tests
- Validate releases and smoke-test critical flows
- Track defects and verify fixes

Interactions
- Work with Developers to design testable features
- Provide sign-off on PRs/releases per Definition of Done

---

## Stakeholders

Role Summary
Stakeholders provide inputs, approvals, and domain context for decisions.

Responsibilities
- Provide business context and acceptance
- Review milestones and major deliverables
- Decide on priority and funding where applicable

Interactions
- Receive regular updates from PM and PdM
- Participate in decision gates and milestone reviews

---

## Additional Personas (Proposed)

Scrum Master
- Responsibilities: Facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, coach the team on agile practices, and protect the team from distraction.
- Interactions: Works closely with the PM and PdM to ensure smooth ceremony cadence; acts as escalation coach for process-related issues.

UX Designer
- Responsibilities: Run user research, produce wireframes and prototypes, create accessible and usable designs, and facilitate usability testing.
- Interactions: Collaborates with PdM on requirements, hands off designs to Developers, and works with QA to validate usability acceptance criteria.

Data Analyst
- Responsibilities: Define success metrics, instrument metrics & dashboards, analyze outcomes, and produce insights for prioritization and retrospectives.
- Interactions: Partners with PdM to set metrics, supports QA with test data requirements, and provides PMs with progress reports and dashboards.

Release Manager
- Responsibilities: Maintain release schedule, own deployment checklists, coordinate cross-team releases, and own rollback/incident coordination for deployments.
- Interactions: Works with PMs, Developers, and QA during release windows and with stakeholders for release communications.

DevOps / SRE (if separate)
- Responsibilities: Maintain CI/CD pipelines, observability, and runbook readiness.
- Interactions: Works with Release Manager for deployments and with Developers for production readiness.

Business Analyst (optional)
- Responsibilities: Translate stakeholder needs into clear requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Interactions: Facilitates requirements refinement between PdM and Developers.

---

## How to use these personas
- Use the persona definitions to assign clear ownership for activities and artifacts.
- Add persona responsibilities to role assignments in project one-pagers and sprint plans.
- Use these descriptions during onboarding to reduce ambiguity and speed ramp-up.
